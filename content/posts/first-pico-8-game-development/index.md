I followed a wonderful [tutorial](https://youtu.be/Yu-FUnu0pXk?si=77tGdU-uCP6_pvGV) by [SpaceCat](https://www.youtube.com/@spacecat88) and created my first [PICO-8](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php) game.

<!--more-->

Shits are falling from the sky. Move the toilet bowl to catch as much shit as possible. This is a very simple game. It covers the basics of Lua sprite & map design and collision detection. Nothing fanciful, but it makes a good programming practice and I have fun while learning how to develop a game.
